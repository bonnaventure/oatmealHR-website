*Last updated: October 8, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how OatmealHR ("OatmealHR," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards personal information when you visit our website, request materials such as a sample hiring report, use our candidate screening services as an employer client, or complete an assessment as a job candidate. Please read it carefully. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of our site and services.

#### Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run a concierge-style behavioral screening service:

- **Business contact information.** When you book a demo, start a pilot, or contact us, we collect your name, work email, company, role you are hiring for, applicant volume, timeline, and any message you send.
- **Candidate information.** With the instruction of our employer clients, candidates invited to an assessment provide basic identifying details (name, email, role applied for) and their written responses to assessment questions. Candidates are informed that their responses will be analyzed using AI tools and reviewed by humans before a report is delivered to the employer.
- **Website and usage data.** We collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referring site, directly or via analytics providers and cookies (see below).

We do **not** intentionally collect protected characteristics such as race, gender, age, religion, disability status, or sexual orientation, and our assessments are designed so scoring does not rely on such information.

#### How We Use Your Information

- To deliver ranked shortlists, individual candidate profiles, and recommended interview questions to our employer clients.
- To communicate about demos, pilots, support requests, and billing.
- To improve our assessment questions, scoring rubrics, and reports.
- To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use candidate responses to train third-party general-purpose AI models. AI analysis is performed on our behalf under confidentiality and data-processing terms, and every report is reviewed by a human before delivery.

#### Sharing of Information

We share personal data only with: employer clients participating in a pilot or engagement (candidate profiles and rankings); service providers such as hosting, email, and analytics vendors bound by data-protection obligations; professional advisors; and where required by law, court order, or governmental request. If we are involved in a merger or acquisition, customer data may be transferred as part of that transaction, and we will notify you where the law requires it.

#### Protection of Personal Information

We use organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including encryption in transit, access controls limited to personnel who need the data to perform their role, logging, and periodic review of our practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work hard to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

#### Data Retention and Deletion

Employer account and inquiry data is retained while your account or engagement is active and thereafter as needed for legitimate business and legal purposes. Candidate assessment responses are retained for a limited period following report delivery so clients can review and validate findings, after which they are deleted or anonymized. Candidates may request access to or deletion of their responses at any time by contacting us; note that we may retain information where legally required or to resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

#### Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to make the site work and non-essential analytics cookies to understand traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

#### Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business users and adult job candidates. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

#### International Transfers

If you access the site from a region with laws or regulations governing personal data collection different than those of the United States, those laws apply to processing of your data, and we take steps such as standard contractual clauses where appropriate.

#### Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on this page with an updated revision date; significant changes affecting candidates or clients will additionally be communicated by email where we hold contact details.
