faqs:
  - title: How fast will we receive our ranked shortlist?
    answer: Most pilots are delivered within 48 hours of launch. Role setup and candidate invitations happen the same day; candidates typically have a 24–48 hour window to complete the assessment, and analysis plus human review takes another 4–24 hours after responses come in. Larger applicant pools or multiple roles may take a bit longer—we'll confirm the timeline before your pilot starts.

  - title: Do you offer discounts for startups, non-profits, or educational institutions?
    answer: "Yes—early-stage startups and registered non-profits can apply for reduced pilot pricing, and we run free pilots with select workforce-development and educational programs. Reach out via our [contact page](/contact) with a little background on your organization and hiring needs, and we'll let you know what we can offer."

  - title: Can we customize the assessment questions for our role?
    answer: Team Pilot and Agency Pilot plans support role-specific trait weighting, so the ranking reflects what matters most for the position you're hiring for (for example, emphasizing reliability for early-shift roles). Fully custom question sets are available for recurring and enterprise engagements. The core four traits—competence, hard work, trustworthiness, and reliability—stay consistent so you can still compare candidates fairly across roles.

  - title: Is there documentation and support available?
    answer: Every pilot includes a review call where we walk your hiring team through the report, plus a guide explaining the question types, the scoring framework, and how to use the recommended interview questions. Between pilots, you can email [hello@oatmealhr.com](mailto:hello@oatmealhr.com) and we typically respond within one business day. Our [pricing page](/pricing) and this FAQ cover most process questions up front.

  - title: What is the refund policy?
    answer: If we deliver your pilot and you feel it didn't provide value, tell us within 14 days of report delivery and we'll refund your pilot fee in full—no lengthy negotiation required. If fewer than half of your invited candidates complete the assessment, we'll either re-run the screening with a fresh candidate group at no charge or refund the difference. See the [terms and conditions](/terms-conditions) for the complete policy.

  - title: Will an AI decide who gets rejected?
    answer: No. OatmealHR is decision support, not a decision maker. AI helps analyze responses against a structured rubric, but a human reviews every report before delivery, and nothing is ever automatically rejected based on a score. Candidates advance—or don't—based on choices made by your hiring team. You can read more about how we handle AI, bias awareness, and candidate data on our [FAQ](/faq) and in our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
