2. Target Audiences
Primary Audience
Hiring managers and HR leaders at small to mid-sized businesses who are overwhelmed by applicant volume and need to make faster shortlisting decisions.
They care about:
- Speed.
- Reliability.
- Reducing bad hires.
- Avoiding wasted interviews.
- Getting a practical shortlist.
- Not needing another complicated software tool.

Secondary Audience
Recruiting agencies and staffing firms that need to screen candidates quickly and prove value to clients.
They care about:
- Speed to shortlist.
- Candidate quality.
- White-label options.
- Client-ready reporting.
- Scalable screening.

Tertiary Audience
Operations leaders, founders, and team leads who are hiring for roles where reliability, trust, and work ethic matter more than pedigree.
They care about:
- Who will show up.
- Who can be trusted.
- Who follows through.
- Who can handle responsibility.

8. The Assessment Page Outline
Purpose: Explain the questionnaire in a way that feels intelligent, human, and not creepy.
Primary CTA: See Sample Questions

Section 1: Hero
Headline: Questions candidates cannot rehearse.
Subheadline: Instead of asking candidates to repeat their resume, OatmealHR asks practical, open-ended questions that reveal how they think, plan, and take responsibility.

Section 2: Why Lateral Thinking?
Headline: Rehearsed answers hide real behavior.
Body: Most candidates know how to answer “What is your greatest weakness?” or “Tell me about a challenge you overcame.” Lateral-thinking questions make it harder to rely on scripted responses. They reveal structure, clarity, honesty, and practical judgment.

Section 3: Sample Questions
Headline: Simple questions. Useful signals.
Examples:
- How do you make a bowl of oatmeal? *(Brand Synergy Note: This perfectly ties into the OatmealHR name!)*
- What kind of alarm clock do you use?
- If your reliability were a vehicle, what kind of engine would it have?
- Describe the color blue using only temperature and texture.
- You need to move a pile of sand with a teaspoon. What do you do in hour one versus hour ten?
Note: There are no trick questions. We are looking for clarity, preparation, accountability, and practical thinking.

Section 4: What Each Question Reveals
Headline: What we look for.
| Question Type | What It Helps Reveal |
| --- | --- |
| Simple process questions | Planning, clarity, attention to detail |
| Preparation questions | Reliability, conscientiousness |
| Persistence questions | Work ethic, resilience |
| Ethical metaphor questions | Trustworthiness, accountability |
| Abstract communication questions | Communication, adaptability |

Section 5: Scoring Approach
Headline: Structured scoring, not gut feeling.
Body: Each response is evaluated for evidence related to four traits: Competence, Hard Work, Trustworthiness, Reliability. Scores are role-weighted and supported by evidence from the candidate’s responses.

Section 6: Candidate Experience
Headline: A better experience for candidates too.
Points: Short and engaging, No right or wrong answers, No need to prepare, Mobile-friendly, Respectful of candidate time, Optional transparency summary.
